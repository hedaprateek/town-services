Changes here go nowhere until you press Publish. The live page is Local Services & Help ↗.

The list

Only Name has to be filled in. Category is the big heading a contact sits under, Role is the trade and becomes a smaller heading inside it, and Covers is the two or three words shown under the name — “small works”, “routine jobs”, the areas they serve.

How the page looks

The caution is also attached to every contact anyone shares from the page, so the warning travels with the number.

Sponsored slots

Paid placements. They are kept out of the list itself — a sponsor is never counted in the header, never returned by a search, and never shared as a number a neighbour gave — and every one is labelled Sponsored on the page. Slot is where it appears: top above the list, feed between the sections, footer as a name at the bottom. Link must start with https://. Until is the date the slot is paid up to (YYYY-MM-DD) — after it, the slot stops showing on its own.

Leave that blank and the page shows no advertising of any kind. Fill it in and any slot nobody has bought yet becomes a quiet “Advertise here” card pointing at you.
